## Origin message callsite research

The first 270135 candidate query at `0x353A8CC` was installed and fingerprinted, but no self-revoke event entered its helper. Disassembly of the actual revoke entry at `0x31E54D4` shows its inner handler calls `0x35373C8` at `0x31E55BC`. The outer hook sits immediately afterward at `0x31E55C0`. A live test showed it receives a type-10000 system row, so the inner handler's original-message query at `0x3537F98` now has a second hook at `0x3537F9C`. The old DeleteMessages entry `0x2859744` maps by a unique 40-byte entry to `0x31B2294`; its hook remains disabled.

The outer query writes a 640-byte temporary at `sp+0x50` and reads its optional flag at `sp+0x2C8`. The outer hook replays `LDR X20,[SP,#0x2D8]`, `CBZ`, `ADD X8,X20,#8`, `MOV X9,#-1`. The inner query writes a 640-byte temporary at `sp+0x38` and reads its optional flag at `sp+0x2B0`; its hook replays the corresponding `X22` sequence and clears the flag only for a valid non-system origin. Live verification of inner-hook execution, row retention, notice content and forwarding remains required.
